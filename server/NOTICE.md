# Third-Party Data Attribution

## MACULA Hebrew Linguistic Datasets (CC BY 4.0)

https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-hebrew/

- Syntax trees: Clear Bible, Inc. + Groves Center
- Morphology: Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (MorphHB)
- Glosses: Cherith Glosses by Andi Wu (CC BY 4.0)
- Note: SDBH columns excluded — permission-based license, not open.

## STEPBible TBESH/TBESG Lexicon (CC BY 4.0)

https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data

- TBESH: Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Hebrew
- TBESG: Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Greek
- ~20K lexicon entries with glosses, transliterations, and meanings

## UBS/Paratext Versification Data (MIT)

https://github.com/ubsicap/versification_json

- Hebrew-English versification mapping (eng.json)
- ~144 canonical verse numbering differences

## OpenBible.info Cross-References (CC BY 4.0)

https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/

- ~340K editorial tradition cross-reference verse pairs
- Community-voted association strength
